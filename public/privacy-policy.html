Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

1. Information We Collect

DarkNight Fitness collects the following information when you register:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Attendance Records (check-in and check-out times)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To manage your gym membership and attendance
  • To send attendance notifications and updates
  • To improve our services and user experience
  • To communicate important gym announcements

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: For secure data storage and management
  • Brevo (Sendinblue): For sending email notifications
  • Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Keep you signed in to your account
  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze site traffic and usage
  • Display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction. Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request corrections to your data
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Opt out of email communications
  • Opt out of personalized ads

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

9. Google AdSense and DoubleClick Cookie

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of the DoubleClick cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of the use of the DoubleClick cookie for interest-based advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page.
